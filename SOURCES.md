## FINRA Rules

### Regulatory Element CE — Annual Cycle
- **Rule:** FINRA Rule 1240(a)
- **Change:** Moved from milestone-based (2nd anniversary + every 3 years) to **annual**, due December 31 each year
- **Effective:** January 1, 2023
- **Source:** FINRA Regulatory Notice 21-41
- **URL:** https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/21-41
- **Verify on:** https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/1240

### IPO Research Quiet Period (10-day minimum for all IPO participants)
- **Rule:** FINRA Rule 2241(b)(2)(I)
- **What it says:** All member firms participating as underwriters or dealers in an IPO must establish a quiet period of **at least 10 calendar days** following the IPO effective date. A **3-day** minimum applies to managers/co-managers of a secondary offering.
- **Note:** Prior to the 2015 amendment, managers faced 40 days and non-managing underwriters 25 days. The 2015 rule unified IPO participants at a 10-day minimum.
- **Effective:** June 7, 2015
- **Source:** FINRA Regulatory Notice 15-30
- **URL:** https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/15-30
- **Verify on:** https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/2241

### Form U4 Amendment Deadlines
- **Rule:** FINRA Rule 1122; FINRA By-Laws Article V, Section 2
- **What it says:** 30 calendar days from awareness for most events; certain events (criminal charges, regulatory actions) have accelerated deadlines
- **Verify on:** https://www.finra.org/registration-exams-ce/classic-crd/form-u4

### Regulation Best Interest (primary standard for retail recommendations)
- **Rule:** FINRA Rule 2111 now applies primarily to institutional customers; Reg BI governs retail
- **Verify current FINRA guidance:** https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/key-topics/regulation-best-interest

### Gifts & Gratuities (Rule 3220 — $100 limit)
- **Rule:** FINRA Rule 3220 (gifts by associated persons to employees of other member firms)
- **Verify on:** https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/3220

### Outside Business Activities
- **Rule:** FINRA Rule 3270
- **Verify on:** https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/3270

### All current FINRA rules (rulebook)
- **URL:** https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules

### Recent FINRA Regulatory Notices (check for updates after January 2026)
- **URL:** https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices

## SEC Rules

### T+1 Settlement — SEC Rule 15c6-1(a)
- **Change:** Equities, corporate bonds, and municipal bonds moved from T+2 to **T+1**
- **Effective:** May 28, 2024
- **SEC Release:** 34-96930
- **URL:** https://www.sec.gov/rules/final/2023/34-96930.pdf
- **Verify on:** https://www.sec.gov/investor/alerts/T+1settlementfaq.htm

### Regulation Best Interest — SEC Rule 15l-1
- **Effective:** June 30, 2020
- **SEC Release:** 34-86031
- **URL:** https://www.sec.gov/rules/final/2019/34-86031.pdf
- **Verify on:** https://www.sec.gov/regulation-best-interest

### ⚠️ Qualified Client Thresholds — SEC Rule 205-3 (Investment Advisers Act)
- **Current thresholds (as of Nov 2021 SEC order):** Net worth > $2.2 million OR ≥ $1.1 million AUM with adviser
- **Adjusted periodically** by SEC order — verify the current figure
- **URL:** https://www.sec.gov/rules/final/2021/ia-5756.pdf
- **Verify on:** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-275/section-275.205-3

### IPO Quiet Period — Research Analyst Conflicts
- **SEC Rule 139** (research safe harbors); FINRA Rule 2241 governs the specific day counts
- **Verify on:** https://www.sec.gov/rules/final/2005/33-8591.pdf

### Schedule 13D — 5 Business Day Filing Deadline
- **Change:** Reduced from 10 calendar days to **5 business days**
- **Effective:** February 5, 2024
- **SEC Release:** 34-97144
- **URL:** https://www.sec.gov/rules/final/2023/34-97144.pdf

### Regulation A+ Tier Limits (Tier 1: $20M, Tier 2: $75M)
- **Effective for $75M cap:** March 15, 2021
- **Verify on:** https://www.sec.gov/smallbusiness/exemptofferings/rega

### All SEC final rules
- **URL:** https://www.sec.gov/rules/final.shtml

## IRS / Retirement Account Limits

### ⚠️ IRA Contribution Limits (annually adjusted)
- **2023:** $6,500 base / $7,500 with catch-up (age 50+)
- **2024:** $7,000 base / $8,000 with catch-up
- **2026:** $7,500 base / $8,600 with catch-up (per IRS News Release IR-2025-111)
- **Verify current year:** https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-ira-contribution-limits

### ⚠️ 401(k) Contribution Limits (annually adjusted)
- **Verify current year:** https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-401k-and-profit-sharing-plan-contribution-limits

### Required Minimum Distributions — SECURE 2.0
- **RMD start age:** 73 for those born 1951–1959; 75 for those born 1960+
- **Law:** SECURE 2.0 Act of 2022
- **Verify on:** https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-plan-and-ira-required-minimum-distributions-faqs

### ERISA Vesting — Matching Contributions (2-to-6 year graded schedule)
- **Law:** Pension Protection Act of 2006 (amended IRC §411)
- **Verify on:** https://www.dol.gov/general/topic/retirement/typesofplans

## FTC / Antitrust

### ⚠️ HSR Premerger Notification Thresholds (adjusted every February)
- **2024 size-of-transaction threshold:** $119.5 million (effective February 5, 2024)
- **2025 size-of-transaction threshold:** $126.4 million
- **2026 size-of-transaction threshold:** $133.9 million (effective February 2026)
- Adjusted annually by the FTC each February — always verify before applying to a transaction
- **Verify current thresholds:** https://www.ftc.gov/enforcement/premerger-notification-program/hsr-threshold-adjustments-and-reportability

## CFTC / NFA (Series 3)

### CFTC Front-Running Prohibition — §155.3 (not §155.2)
- **§155.2** = Trading against customer orders (accommodation trading)
- **§155.3** = Trading ahead of customer orders
- **Verify on:** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-I/part-155

### SOFR Futures (replaced Eurodollar futures, which ceased April 2023)
- **CME SOFR futures (SR3):** https://www.cmegroup.com/markets/interest-rates/stirs/three-month-sofr.html
- **Eurodollar cessation:** Ceased trading April 14, 2023

## NASAA (Series 63 / 65 / 66)

### Series 63 Statute of Limitations (1956 USA Section 410(e))
- **Standard:** 2 years from discovery, 3 years from transaction date
- **Verify on:** https://www.nasaa.org/industry-resources/corporation-finance/uniform-securities-act/

### ⚠️ Series 65/66 Qualified Client Thresholds
- Same as SEC Rule 205-3 above — check NASAA model rules for state-level adoption
- **Verify on:** https://www.nasaa.org/industry-resources/investment-advisers/

### All NASAA model rules and exam content outlines
- **URL:** https://www.nasaa.org/exams/
